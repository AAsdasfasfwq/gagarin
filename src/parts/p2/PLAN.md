# P2 · 155.00–305.72 · "Launch Site 41" — director's plan

## Concept — "The Fuse"
P1 ends on two droplets (UDMH chartreuse + nitric-acid amber) that spark on contact. P2 is that spark's
fuse burning for 70 seconds: a night-time thriller at a floodlit pad where every safe option is closed one
by one (rulebook → map → calendar → a marshal's order → a chair 56 ft from the bomb), until the fuse reaches
the tanks and the same two colours meet again — this time as an ocean of fire. The second half is the
opposite film: restraint. No gore; the dead are present only as **absences** — shadows left on concrete,
footprints that stop at a pool, an empty chair, a melted star, 126 memorial flames — and then the state
paints over even that (stamp, telegram, redaction bar) and fails: smoke cannot be classified. The young
cosmonauts look at the soot, the rocket becomes a dragon, and the smoke settles into a black scorch circle
that P3 turns into the Vostok sphere.

**Motifs used:** trajectory ink-line = the electrical signal / fuse / flame path / running footprints /
plane→rocket morph / dragon spine; circles = clock dial → panel dial (mask match) → fireball core → scorch
circle (seam); classified paper (stamp СОВЕРШЕННО СЕКРЕТНО, telegram, redaction); telemetry tags `[ … ]`;
the clock (OCT 23 → NOV 7 deadline → 18:45); the orange dot appears only with Gagarin (283 s +).

## Colour states
- **night** 155–209: night-blue ground `#101A2E`, sodium-gold floodlights, cream ink, cyan diagnostic HUD,
  red = fault/deadline, toxic chartreuse/amber = propellants.
- **disaster** 209–241: panel amber → fire ramp; soot black only seconds before the blast and inside the
  "HELL ON EARTH" knockout (always something alive: embers, heat shimmer).
- **ash** 241–270 & 291–306: warm grey `#3A3733`, falling ash flakes as the living layer, single cold light.
- **archive** 268–283: paper cream, ink, vermilion stamp — cold typewriter rhythm.
