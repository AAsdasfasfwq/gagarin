# P6 · "One Orbit" · 817.94 – 972.42 s (154.48 s)

## Visual concept
**The orbit is a clock, and the clock is a trap.** P6 opens on the night Earth P5 hands us and pulls out
until the film's ink-line spine becomes the orbit ellipse — then that ellipse grows 108 minute-ticks and
turns into a dial. From then on every idea is one of three circles: the **dial** (time: 108 min, 5–7 vs
20 days, 10:25, the 1-second-short burn, 30°/s), the **sphere** (Gagarin's capsule, its porthole, its
helmet) and the **Earth** (the thing he must get back to). The orange dot — Gagarin — rides the ink line
through all of it; when he is in danger the line goes amber, when he is hypothetically lost the dot greys.
Wonder (indigo, celesta) → a tiny comic breath in the cabin (the pencil gets its own mini-trajectory) →
the floor drops out: a cold "warning" engineering world (slate ground, amber accent, mono telemetry) that
tightens like a mechanism until the spacecraft is literally spinning and the porthole fills with fire.

Style mix: 2D editorial/infographic ink world on textured grounds (orbit dials, rulers, gauges, plan
animatic) · 2.5D cartoon set with parallax + DOF (cabin, carousel, porthole, tightrope, physics drawing) ·
true 3D Three.js Vostok (sphere + biconic instrument module, gas-bottle ring, straps, cable bundle) for
the separation shots. Approx: 2D 50 % · 2.5D 32 % · 3D 18 %.
