# P4 · "The Night Before" — 477.08–649.76 (172.68 s)

## Visual concept
A nocturne that turns into a dawn and then into a machine. One idea carries the whole part:
**the line** — the rule line of the date card becomes the steppe horizon; the horizon becomes the
ink of the farewell letter; the ink becomes the ECG trace of the sleeping cosmonaut (and Korolev's
anxious loop around the house); the calm pulse flattens into the dawn horizon; the horizon becomes
the road of the bus; the wheel hub becomes the R-7's engine circle; and finally a ring of 30 nuts
around the closed hatch becomes a clock dial ticking the last minutes.
Light = life: one warm tungsten source (desk lamp, lamp post, cigarette ember) against the cold
blue night; at dawn THE ORANGE arrives (the suit) and the world turns warm coral; at the pad the
palette goes cold steel + LOX white, and the part ends on one small green light (contact 32).

Colour states: `night` (477–570: night blue #101A2E, cream, tungsten gold) → `dawn` (570–600: coral
sky, ink, orange) → steel/concrete morning (600–628) → near-black `warning` silence (628–633) →
steel + one green lamp (633–649.76).
Mix: 2D ≈ 50 % (letter macro, memory vignettes, plan views, schematics, hatch, terrain), 2.5D ≈ 35 %
(Cam25 night steppe, desk, cottage dollhouse, Korolev window, bus road, goodbye), 3D ≈ 15 % (R-7 on
the pad, Three.js). Characters ≈ 18 % (letter, cottage, Korolev, goodbye, Ivanovsky).

## Music arc (D/G world, P3 hands over on D major)
hushed single low piano D (477.08) + steppe wind → intimate piano + solo cello for the letter
(D major / B minor, motif fragment at "Valyusha", swell at "I loved you all very much") →
suspended drone + tubular bell for the 7-year jump, near-silence at the death line, lamp click →
night clock tick + soft heartbeat for the cottage → anxious clarinet + pizz ostinato for Korolev
(drips, lighter) → calm celesta pulse for the perfect pulse → fresh dawn strings + flute (G→D),
bell at 05:30, comic squish for the tubes, horn swell for THE ORANGE, radar beeps for the reticle →
cheeky bassoon/pizz for the bus & wheel → awe: low brass + choir + timpani at the R-7, LOX hiss,
noble horn motif for the goodbye, servo elevator, hatch clank → mechanical thriller ticking (120 bpm)
for contact 32, buzzer, sub drop on "killing" → ABSOLUTE SILENCE (628.44) only valve hiss →
ratchet frenzy for the 30 nuts → release D-major chord + shimmer when the light comes on → a lone
clock tick for "minutes remain" that stops exactly at 649.76 (P5 owns the downbeat).
