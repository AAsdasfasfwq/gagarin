# P1 · "The Arms Race" · 0.00–155.00 s — Director's plan

## Visual concept
**"A dream smuggled inside a weapon."** P1 is a cold machine world drawn in engraved steel, blueprint
ink and Soviet print. The film opens on a *steel-engraved globe* frosting over; the two blocs face
each other across the Arctic (the real polar war-room projection) bristling with warheads; the
ballistic ink-arcs (film spine motif #1) cross the pole and the planet burns to ash. The ash rises
and becomes stars — the romantic space poster we all remember — which is **torn** to reveal the
military blueprint underneath. From there everything is a weapon: an orbit snaps into a ballistic
arc, a warhead drops on chains, a race against the button. Korolev is the one warm human light in
the hangar: he hides a glowing dream (with the orange dot) inside the nose cone. Persuasion,
propaganda, the 3D R-7, then the courage of the people who will ride it — and a rewind to the
secret: October 1960, Tyuratam, the R-16, Yangel, a deadline, haste, and the chemistry that ends
in two droplets meeting at frame centre.

Recurring devices: ink trajectory (cream, orange head) · the orange dot (Gagarin / the human, from
frame 1: a tiny warm point near Moscow on the frozen globe) · circles for match-cuts (red pulse →
button → lamp; timeline dot → dossier) · classified paper · mono telemetry tags.

## Music arc (D-minor world)
cold dread drone + clock ostinato (0–17) → fragile celesta Poyekhali motif in D major (romance,
18–23) → tape-stop + minor brass stab (tear / ARMS RACE) → military ostinato + snare, ticking race to
the button (30–47) → hush, cello + glass motif hint for "dream" (48–57) → sly pizzicato/bassoon for
the politburo (57–65) → pompous propaganda brass fanfare (65–71) → hush/heartbeat (71–76) → epic
low brass + choir + arp (R-7, 76–88) → three dissonant hits (88–92) → solo horn motif fragment
(courage) → reverse rewind → dark typewriter/stamp + ominous cluster ("monstrous") → tubular bell
date accent → steppe wind, low drone, horn (R-16) → pizzicato pressure → frantic accelerating
ostinato, hard freeze on "paralyzing" → toxic detuned phrygian synths, bubbling → riser peaking at
155.0 (no downbeat — P2 owns it).
