# P5 · "Poyekhali!" — 649.76 → 817.94 (168.2 s) — PEAK 2

## Visual concept — "The altitude is the clock"
One man, one vertical line. The part is built on the **trajectory ink-line** standing up: it is born as a
radio waveform in Gagarin's headphones, becomes the thrust staircase of Korolev's commands, shoots up as
the ascent path, is crushed flat by g-force, carries the staging milestones through the atmosphere layers,
flat-lines into the ringing silence of engine cutoff, and finally bends into the **curvature of the Earth**.
Every scene's sky is driven by one function of altitude (`skyAt(alt)`): dawn coral on the pad → spring blue →
high-altitude cobalt → indigo → space. So even across hard cuts the viewer *feels* the climb.
Circles carry the match cuts (clock → key lamp → nozzle → porthole → Earth). Orange is only Gagarin: his suit,
the head of the trajectory, the word ПОЕХАЛИ's underline, the strike-through of the doctors' fear.

Emotional curve: intimate lull (love song) → mechanical countdown → roar (liftoff) → held breath (bunker) →
human voice → **LET'S GO** (biggest type moment of the film) → crushing, driving ascent → light (fairing off,
sunlight) → acceleration to the edge → **hard cut to absolute silence** → floating wonder → first sight of Earth.

## Colour states
`night` (capsule, tungsten/green instrument glow) → `dawn` (pad, coral sky, 09:07) → `ascent` (sky → cobalt,
fire accents) → `warning`-dark for the g-force interiors (slate + amber gauge light) → `inkDeep` black-indigo for
cutoff silence (grain + one mote always alive) → `orbit` (space indigo, halo cyan, cream) → night-side Earth.
