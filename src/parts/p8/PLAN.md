# P8 · "One of You" · 1127.00–1306.00 — director's plan

## Visual concept
**The orange dot comes home — and becomes everyone's.** P8 travels outward in scale and then
back to one face: a potato field (two women, one man from the sky) → a nation (radio rings,
streets filling like liquid) → the world (routes, front pages, a queen's photograph) → the cage
of fame (a glass vitrine, a ring of grey hats, a bronze monument that blinks) → the silence of
March 1968 (a contrail that simply stops) → the finale, where the 108-minute orbit unrolls into
the **dividing line**, the line becomes the **horizon**, and the orange dot launches one last
time into the final title **LET'S GO**.

Recurring devices: the trajectory ink-line (descent arc → course of history → radio rings →
tour routes → contrail → orbit → horizon) · circles (helmet opening, radio rings, light hole,
globe, ring of officials, orbit, sun) · THE ORANGE (only Gagarin: suit, the dot, his route).

Craft rules for this part: textured grounds everywhere (mode-7 textured perspective field,
paper, linen, brass, bronze, glass), real perspective (local projector for ground planes),
characters acting in depth (backing away in z, heavy walk toward lens, rack focus), one hero per
frame, 1–5 words on screen, word-synced.

## Colour states
spring (field, morning, soil + sky + suit orange) → triumph (red dusk, gold fireworks,
cream placards) → dark newsprint (fear) → burn to warm light → editorial paper (fame, queen) →
coda dusk violet / bronze / cold glass (burden) → grey-violet snow (1968) → indigo + dawn coral
(finale), resolving on warm dawn horizon + orange dot.

## Music arc (D major world; quiet under VO)
folk plucks + balalaika (field, G→D) · sly bassoon/pizz + woodblock (terror, spy sting with
muted brass, alien "theremin" solo) · warm motif hint on balalaika ("one of you") · timpani swell
("changed history") · radio-bus fanfare (Levitan) · festive march: snare, brass, tubular bells,
crowd (celebration / Victory Day) · dark low strings dip (fear) → choir + shimmer (light) ·
rising strings (humanity) · flashbulb pizz ostinato (fame) · travelling glock/arp (tour) ·
elegant 3/4 waltz (the Queen) · detuned waltz → solo cello (burden) · cold low ostinato + ticks
(guarded, ring of officials) · bronze bell hit (monument) · stamp rhythm (years) · lift (sky) →
near-silence, one tubular bell, solo piano (1968, 34) · celesta "108" callback · pad build ·
taiko montage hits · strings+choir build · the motif climbs and lands its octave ON "let's go"
(horns, strings, timpani, cymbal) → resolution & tail to ~1308.
