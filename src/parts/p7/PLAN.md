# P7 · "Fire and Earth" · 972.42 – 1127.00 (154.58 s) — PEAK 3

## Visual concept
**A falling ember that cools into a man.** The window opens inside the inferno: the Vostok sphere is a
meteor wrapped in plasma, and every beat of the part is a step of *cooling* — from 17,400 mph plasma
(ember/plasma ramp) to the ice-calm of one human face (a cold cyan frost pushing the fire back), to the
charred sphere in thin blue air, to an ironic paper world (the FAI cover-up), to white canopies in a
spring sky, and finally to the soil of a plowed field. Temperature is the colour script; the orange
of Gagarin's suit is the one constant (the orange dot rides inside every diagram).
Motifs: the **trajectory ink-line** becomes the descent path, then the branching "plan" (sphere vs man),
then the parachute lines; **circles**: shock crescent → porthole → g-meter dial → the roly-poly sphere →
the canopy → the visor → the map pin; **classified paper**: redaction bar & stamps for the FAI lie.

Mix: 3D (Three.js Vostok sphere + instrument module + cable, real lighting) ≈ 12 %, 2.5D (Cam25 planes,
capsule interior, sky falls, parachutes) ≈ 40 %, 2D (diagrams, kinetic type, paper world, pulp comic,
map) ≈ 48 %. Motion graphics/infographics/type ≥ 60 %.

## Colour states
`reentry` (ember ground #2A0E10, PLASMA_RAMP) → `warning`/ice (cool slate + cyan frost) → `reentry`
→ sky (`ascent` reversed: indigo → day blue) → `blueprint`-on-paper (`editorial`) → `archive` (FAI) →
`spring` sky → `spring` field (soil, morning light) = seam to P8.
