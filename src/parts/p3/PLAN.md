# P3 · "The Sphere and the Chosen One" · 305.72–477.08 (171.4 s)

## Concept — two circles
The part is a journey between two circles: a **cold engineered sphere** (Vostok's descent ball, drawn
in blueprint ink) and a **warm human smile** (Gagarin's face radiating light). The machine is
explained first — its shape, skin, guts and its distrust of the human inside it — and then the film
turns around and explains why *this* human was trusted with it. Colour travels with the idea:
`blueprint` (navy + cyan ink, the orange only on the human) → `editorial` paper for the doctors' fears
(ironic, clinical) → amber nixie glow for the secret code → paper/ink for the selection → a warm,
lamp-lit centrifuge hall for the comedy → gold newsprint light for the smile. The **orange dot**
(Gagarin) starts as a tiny figure beside the sphere and ends filling the frame as light.

Recurring devices inside the part: the circle (sphere → porthole → loupe → nixie glow → spirit-level
bubble → centrifuge from above → smile), the plumb line (gravity / stability), the
pilots' forced grins (lie) vs Gagarin's pale honest face.

the smile, shutter, paper slaps of front pages, soft swell resolving D major into 477.08 (no hit —
